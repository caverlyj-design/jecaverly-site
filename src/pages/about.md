### Jonathan E. Caverly
*Public Servant | Emergency Manager | EMT | Freemason | Lifelong Student*

I'm John Caverly, a husband, emergency management professional, Emergency Medical Technician, elected fire commissioner, Freemason, and lifelong student of just about anything that catches my curiosity.

I've spent much of my adult life involved in public service, emergency response, technology, and community organizations. My professional and volunteer experiences have taken me from the back of an ambulance to emergency operations centers, hospital emergency management programs, incident command posts, and public governing boards.

Along the way, I've learned that leadership isn't necessarily about rank, titles, or being the loudest person in the room. It's about responsibility, preparation, judgment, and being willing to do the work when something needs doing.

## Public Service and Emergency Management

Emergency management and public safety have been central to my professional life.

I've worked in emergency medical services, healthcare emergency management, occupational health and safety, and disaster preparedness. My experience includes coordinating emergency management programs in healthcare, providing field medical support, teaching, and supporting emergency response operations.

I currently serve as an elected Fire Commissioner for Central Pierce Fire & Rescue in Washington State. I also serve with Okanogan County Fire District 12, where I've been involved in emergency medical services, training, and organizational development.

My background includes service in the Civil Air Patrol, including command responsibilities and involvement in emergency services.

I believe public agencies exist to serve their communities, and that good governance requires transparency, accountability, fiscal responsibility, and a willingness to ask difficult questions.

Public service is a responsibility, not a personal entitlement.

## Freemasonry and Fraternal Service

Freemasonry is another important part of my life.

I'm active in the Masonic fraternity and several of its related organizations, including the York Rite. My interests extend beyond meetings and ritual into Masonic history, symbolism, philosophy, education, and the preservation of our institutional heritage.

I have served in Masonic leadership and trustee positions, including responsibilities involving historic cemetery preservation.

For me, Freemasonry is about more than tradition. It's an ongoing effort to become a better man through education, service, fellowship, and personal reflection.

## Technology, History, and Curiosity

I've always been fascinated by how things work.

My interests include computers, communications systems, amateur radio, artificial intelligence, 3D printing, historical research, and the practical application of technology to everyday problems.

I'm equally comfortable spending an evening studying historical documents, configuring a radio system, building a website, or painting miniature BattleMechs.

I'm a lifelong learner, although my curiosity occasionally creates more projects than any reasonable person should attempt simultaneously.

That hasn't stopped me yet.

## Life Beyond the Work

I'm married to Tina, my partner in life and the person who has tolerated my collection of interests, commitments, and unfinished projects for more than twenty-five years.

Outside of work and public service, I enjoy camping, fishing, baseball, hockey, tabletop gaming, and spending time with friends and family.

I'm a Seattle Mariners, Seahawks, and Kraken fan, which has provided me with years of practice in patience, perseverance, and managing unrealistic expectations.

I also enjoy BattleTech, Dungeons & Dragons, and other tabletop games. They offer a chance to exercise creativity, spend time with friends, and occasionally solve problems with dice instead of committee meetings.

## Why This Website Exists

JECaverly.com is my personal corner of the internet.

It's a place to bring together my interests, research, public service, professional experience, projects, and occasional observations about the world.

Some of what you'll find here will be serious: emergency management, leadership, public administration, historical research, and community service.

Other parts will be considerably less serious: gaming, technology experiments, hobbies, and whatever subject has captured my attention this week.

I don't claim to have all the answers. I've spent enough time in emergency management and public service to know that anyone who thinks they do probably hasn't asked enough questions.

What I do believe is that knowledge should be shared, history should be preserved, public service should mean something, and there's always room to learn.

**Service. Curiosity. History. Responsibility. And occasionally a little sarcasm.**

Welcome to my website.

*John Caverly*
